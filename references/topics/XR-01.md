# XR-01 — XR discovery and device-specific adoption boundary

When: The game targets a headset or tracked controllers.

Native candidates: `XRServer`, `XRInterface`, `XROrigin3D`, `XRCamera3D`, `XRController3D`.

## Approach

Discover the native XR rig and runtime interface before inventing camera/controller tracking; route implementation to target-specific research and device calibration.

A manually driven ordinary camera/controller stack presented as a complete XR integration.

## Pitfalls

- An available interface may fail initialization on the host.
- Tracked origin/camera/controller relationships differ from an ordinary desktop rig.
- This introductory survey does not certify comfort, input profiles or device/export support.

## Verify

- Handle unavailable runtime/device explicitly.
- Verify tracking, interaction and output on the intended headset before acceptance.

## Version and limits

4.7 setup overview inspected. Detailed OpenXR/WebXR, device profiles, passthrough and comfort/performance guidance are not fully surveyed.

Specialist/target behavior needs project-specific research and calibration; also read [coverage boundaries](../discovery.md).
