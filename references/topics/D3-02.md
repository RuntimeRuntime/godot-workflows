# D3-02 — Mesh resources and material ownership

When: A scene needs ordinary reusable visible 3D geometry or per-surface appearance.

Native candidates: `MeshInstance3D`, `Mesh`, `StandardMaterial3D`, `ORMMaterial3D`, `ShaderMaterial`.

## Approach

Instance a Mesh resource through MeshInstance3D; use standard material resources for common PBR needs; assign material on mesh for shared use or node override for instance-local appearance.

Use built-in mesh/material resource behavior before custom surface rendering or shaders.

## Pitfalls

- Mesh-level materials are reused by mesh instances.
- Node overrides have a narrower scope.
- Alpha blending has rendering/shadow limitations.

## Verify

- Check resource sharing and intended per-instance override.
- Inspect transparent and shadow result at runtime.
- Reopen saved scene.

## Version and limits

4.7 material and mesh behavior; renderer compatibility may affect shader/material features.
