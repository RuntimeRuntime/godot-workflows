# D3-09 — Kinematic moving mechanisms and platforms

When: A platform, door, or collider is moved by code/animation and must affect bodies it touches.

Native candidates: `AnimatableBody3D`, `AnimationPlayer`, `AnimationMixer`, `CharacterBody3D`, `CollisionShape3D`.

## Approach

Move an AnimatableBody3D on the physics timeline; use CharacterBody3D or specialized mechanics when more movement response is needed.

Use the built-in animatable kinematic body semantics instead of a manually animated StaticBody3D for moving collision surfaces.

## Pitfalls

- Do not combine AnimatableBody3D movement with PhysicsBody3D.move_and_collide.
- Incorrect update timing can cause carry/jitter issues.

## Verify

- Ride the platform in translation and rotation.
- Check collision synchronization, no tunneling/jitter, and animation update mode.

## Version and limits

4.7 AnimatableBody3D class semantics.
