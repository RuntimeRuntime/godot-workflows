# SH-04 — Pause, process modes, time, timers, and tweens

When: Pause, cooldown, delay, time scaling, periodic work, or script-driven interpolation.

Native candidates: `SceneTree.paused`, `Node.process_mode`, `Timer`, `SceneTreeTimer`, `Tween`, `Engine.time_scale`.

## Approach

Use SceneTree pause and per-node process modes; use Timer/create_timer for countdowns and Tween for dynamically selected property targets. Keep editor-authored sequences in the animation workflow.

Avoid ordinary hand-written countdown loops and reusing completed Tweens; do not assume pause suppresses signals or that time_scale slows audio.

## Pitfalls

- Physics servers stop while paused by default.
- Timer behavior depends on time_scale and process callback.
- Engine.time_scale does not increase physics ticks or adjust audio playback speed.

## Verify

- Pause during a representative scenario and inspect UI/game/physics/audio/timer behavior.
- Exercise cancellation and scene-exit cleanup.
- Run intended time scales and check physics precision.

## Version and limits

Godot 4.7 docs; separate authored animation specifics are covered by another avenue.
