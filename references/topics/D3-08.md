# D3-08 — Physics-simulated rigid objects

When: Forces, impacts, gravity, rotation and contact response should determine motion.

Native candidates: `RigidBody3D`, `CollisionShape3D`, `PhysicsMaterial`.

## Approach

Apply forces/impulses and use _integrate_forces for precise physics-state control; configure mass/material and contact monitoring where needed.

Do not write a parallel movement simulation for standard force-driven props unless requirements call for it.

## Pitfalls

- Repeated transform/velocity writes can make behavior unpredictable.
- Contact signals require monitoring and enough reported contacts.
- Fast small bodies may need continuous collision detection.

## Verify

- Test rest, push, impact and required contact signals.
- Check fast-object collision and backend behavior.

## Version and limits

4.7 class docs; physics backend and project settings are part of the behavior contract.
