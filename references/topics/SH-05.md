# SH-05 — Audio buses and positional playback

When: Music/SFX/UI mixing, category volume settings, spatial audio, or room effects.

Native candidates: `AudioServer`, `AudioBusLayout`, `AudioStreamPlayer`, `AudioStreamPlayer2D`, `AudioStreamPlayer3D`, `AudioListener2D`, `AudioListener3D`, `Area2D`, `Area3D`.

## Approach

Route source playback through named buses/effects; use positional player/listener variants for 2D/3D world sources and non-positional playback for UI/music. Use dB-based bus control for mix categories.

Do not create a bespoke mixer for routine routing; use non-positional playback only when positional sound is not required, and do not use visibility to mute a spatial player. A custom sound pool is valid when measured polyphony/overlap needs it.

## Pitfalls

- Bus references are by name and invalid names fall back to Master.
- Master output above 0 dB can clip.
- The 4.7 Audio streams tutorial is marked WIP/outdated; web sample/stream behavior needs target-specific verification.

## Verify

- Adjust each nominated category and confirm only its route changes.
- Check attenuation/listener position/Area redirection in play.
- Test the relevant export target.

## Version and limits

Godot 4.7; audio streams tutorial explicitly warns its content may be outdated.
