# SH-11 — High-level multiplayer replication

When: Remote peers, gameplay RPCs, replicated properties, network-spawned scenes.

Native candidates: `MultiplayerAPI`, `MultiplayerPeer`, `@rpc`, `MultiplayerSpawner`, `MultiplayerSynchronizer`, `SceneReplicationConfig`.

## Approach

Choose the network model/peer; use RPCs for intentional remote calls, MultiplayerSpawner for authority-controlled spawned scenes, and MultiplayerSynchronizer for explicitly selected properties.

Do not hand-roll routine spawning/property replication without evaluating built-ins; do not assume Resource/Object references synchronize or that high-level networking secures game rules.

## Pitfalls

- Authority, transfer mode, visibility, rate and ownership must be explicit.
- Client-controlled results can enable cheating/desync.
- Web and mobile platform networking capabilities differ.

## Verify

- Run two or more peers and check authority, spawn/despawn, replicated state, visibility, disconnects, and invalid inputs per design.
- Test target platform/export permissions.

## Version and limits

Godot 4.7 docs; Android INTERNET permission and HTML5 protocol/features require target checks.

Untrusted content or security-sensitive protocols require the threat-/protocol-specific follow-up in [discovery](../discovery.md). A topic match does not discharge it. Trusted project-authored assets do not automatically activate that branch.
