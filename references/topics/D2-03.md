# D2-03 — PackedScene scene tiles for rich grid objects

When: Place repeated interactive objects, ambient sound emitters, particle effects or other node-rich objects at tile-aligned positions.

Native candidates: `TileMapLayer`, `TileSet`, `TileSetScenesCollectionSource`, `PackedScene`, `CanvasItem`.

## Approach

Create a reusable CanvasItem-rooted scene, add it to a TileSet scenes collection, then paint that scene tile through the TileMap editor. Account for deferred instantiation: scenes become children after the TileMapLayer enters the tree, at the end of a frame.

Use scene tiles instead of manually maintaining a second coordinate-to-PackedScene spawning system when editor-painted placement is wanted. Use atlas tiles for plain repeated images; each scene tile is instantiated individually and costs more than atlas drawing.

## Pitfalls

- Scene-tile creation is deferred and happens after its TileMapLayer parent; do not assume children exist synchronously during the parent's initialization.
- The scene must extend CanvasItem so the TileMapLayer can position it.
- A scene tile occupies one tile slot; alternate tile IDs identify the scene within its scenes collection.
- Large numbers of scene instances can cost more than ordinary atlas cells.

## Verify

- Save/reopen and verify each painted cell references the intended PackedScene.
- Wait through the deferred creation frame, inspect the resulting child scene and verify its transform, collision, signals and cleanup in play.
- Compare a representative map's instance count and frame behavior before choosing scene tiles for dense decoration.

## Version and limits

The deferred-instantiation behavior and `update_internals()` are documented in 4.7. Validate timing against the actual project version before depending on it.
