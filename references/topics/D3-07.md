# D3-07 — Code-driven character movement

When: A player or NPC has explicit game-authored movement and collision-response rules.

Native candidates: `CharacterBody3D`, `CollisionShape3D`, `Shape3D`, `MeshInstance3D`.

## Approach

Use CharacterBody3D with a fitted collider and visual child; move using move_and_slide or move_and_collide from physics processing, according to desired response.

Use built-in character movement/collision helpers before direct transform motion through the world.

## Pitfalls

- CharacterBody3D is not moved by engine gravity/friction automatically.
- 2D values/axes should not be copied blindly to 3D.
- Slope, floor and platform settings affect results.

## Verify

- Exercise floor, slope, wall and moving-platform cases with intended input.
- Confirm motion uses physics step.

## Version and limits

4.7 CharacterBody3D; the tutorial shares concepts but examples are primarily 2D.
