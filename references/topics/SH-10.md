# SH-10 — Threaded resource loading and scene-tree safety

When: Measured load stalls, streamed content, or background scene/resource work.

Native candidates: `ResourceLoader.load_threaded_request`, `ResourceLoader.load_threaded_get_status`, `ResourceLoader.load_threaded_get`, `Thread`, `Node.call_deferred`.

## Approach

Queue asynchronous ResourceLoader requests, observe completion, and integrate into the active SceneTree on the main thread/deferred path. Worker-built detached scene chunks are possible with care.

Do not mutate the active tree from worker threads, edit shared Resources concurrently, or assume load_threaded_get never blocks. Avoid threads absent a demonstrated need.

## Pitfalls

- Active SceneTree operations are not thread-safe.
- Resource caching/sharing makes concurrent mutation risky.
- Rendering/physics thread-safety requires separate settings and may have limitations.

## Verify

- Observe loading progress and completion without blocking in the requested scenario.
- Test cancellation/reentry/scene exit and safe main-thread integration.

## Version and limits

Godot 4.7 docs; thread safety is subsystem-specific, not global.
