# SH-06 — Localization pipeline

When: Player-facing text/assets must support locale changes or the task affects locale-sensitive UI.

Native candidates: `Translation`, `TranslationServer`, `Control.auto_translate`, `tr()`, `tr_n()`, `CSV translations`, `gettext/PO`.

## Approach

Use project translation imports and TranslationServer; choose CSV or gettext/PO to fit the localization workflow; provide font fallbacks and locale-specific resources as needed.

Avoid sentence-fragment concatenation and hand-built translation lookup when the engine translation pipeline fits; custom localization is valid for deliberate domain-specific requirements.

## Pitfalls

- Font glyph coverage and long translations can break layouts.
- CSV plural/context support is documented since 4.6 and plural strings need explicit tr_n().
- Locale and language are not always interchangeable.

## Verify

- Change to nominated locale and inspect visible text/assets.
- Check plural/context behavior, text expansion, fallback glyphs, and directionality.

## Version and limits

Godot 4.7 docs; CSV plural forms/contexts are called out as since 4.6.
