# D3-10 — Camera3D and collision-aware third-person rig

When: A 3D viewport needs an active camera; third-person camera can be obstructed by world geometry.

Native candidates: `Camera3D`, `Node3D`, `SpringArm3D`, `Shape3D`, `Viewport`.

## Approach

For an orbit rig, compose pivot Node3D -> SpringArm3D -> direct-child Camera3D; tune camera/current status for each viewport.

Use SpringArm3D's sweep for the common camera-wall retraction requirement before custom collision code.

## Pitfalls

- Camera may not be current in a multi-camera or SubViewport setup.
- Built-in camera shape requires direct-child relationship.
- Player collider may self-hit without exclusion.

## Verify

- Check current camera per viewport.
- Orbit near walls and inspect retraction, framing and self-collision.

## Version and limits

4.7 SpringArm3D tutorial; camera and physics APIs should match target pin.
